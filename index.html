🧊 Indian Fridge Storage Cheat Sheet

  • Top Shelves: Leftover Sabzi, Dal, Chutneys, Cooked Rice, Dahi and Paneer.
  • Lower Shelves: Raw Meats, Eggs, Milk packets, Fresh Atta dough.
  • Crisper Drawer: Leafy greens (Palak, Methi, Dhaniya), Green Chillies, Tomatoes, Ginger.
  • Fridge Door: Bottles, Pickles (Achar), Ketchup, Spices (Avoid storing milk packets here!).

📊 Household Survey Insights

Top Wasted Item: Cooked Meals

Survey data reveals leftover cooked rice, dal, and excess roties are the #1 source of household waste.

Primary Cause: Cooking Too Much

Over-estimating meal portions and lack of meal planning cause over 45% of Indian kitchen waste.

Best Solution: Reusing Leftovers

Converting leftover rice into Tadka Rice or leftover sabzi into Parathas eliminates 60%+ of food waste!
